### History of Life on Earth (17)

- The evolution of life on Earth began >3.5 Bya
- 99.999...% of all species that ever lived have gone extinct.
- The Earth has changed over time:
	- The ocean and atmosphere have changed (photosynthesis)
	- Plate tectonics.
	- The existence of environments constructed by organisms (soil; forests)
- Life on Earth has changed through time:
	- Single celled and later multicellular as well
	- ocean and later many colonizations of land
	- the evolution of predation and defense many times
	- the evolution of innovations, physiology, mutualisms, ecosystems.
